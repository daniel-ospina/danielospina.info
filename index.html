Welcome

I'm Daniel Ospina. Through my work I research the future of human organisation, gather and empower communities, envision products, and design social systems and mechanisms.

I'm an instigator at RnDAO, a research-led venture builder and product ecosystem focused on collaboration tech and the future of work. You can read our vision here and about our current ventures here.

Before RnDAO I served as Head of Governance at Aragon, facilitating the design and launch of their DAO, and I have advised and contributed to a handful of Web3 communities. I've been a visiting lecturer at Saïd Business School (Oxford University), turned around a family enterprise by embedding self-management practices, started two companies, run workshops for the likes of Google, BCG and the United Nations — and started as a chef, working my way up through the ranks of Michelin star restaurants.

These experiences have taught me that great ideas can come from anywhere, and that giving them fertile soil starts with building caring relationships and empowering systems.

What I do

01

Research

The future of human organisation. What holds a group of people together once the org chart stops explaining anything — cybernetics, complexity, the commons, and self-determination theory.

02

Facilitation

Organisation design in practice: bringing teams and communities into a room and working through the hard conversations about identity, authority, and change.

03

Gathering

Building and empowering communities — the rituals and entry points that turn a crowd into a group that can act together.

04

Systems & mechanisms

Designing the rules: governance systems, social mechanisms, and incentive structures that make decentralised coordination possible instead of merely aspirational.
