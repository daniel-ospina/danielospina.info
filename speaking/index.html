Speaking

Public speaking.

I'm most comfortable on stage, telling stories and planting seeds.

I love sharing, facilitating conversations, and discussing topics related to the future of work, transforming organisations, communities and belonging, enabling creativity, and adopting new ways of working.

I have experience talking at large-scale festivals and business conferences, through to small workshops and intimate events, and am always up for a new challenge. If you're looking for someone to get people thinking a little differently, challenge what ‘thought leaders’ are saying, and generally get the audience excited about our collective possibilities — get in touch.
